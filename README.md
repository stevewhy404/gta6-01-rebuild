Solmar Bay is an original open-world crime game in the spirit of GTA 6: a sun-soaked coastal city to explore on foot and by car, with traffic and pedestrians, cars to steal and drive, a wanted level and the Solmar Bay PD, five story missions for the fixer Mara Voss, taxi jobs, hidden packages, combat, a HUD with a live minimap, a start screen and saves. Every city, character, vehicle, name, story, artwork and sound is original — nothing from Rockstar Games or any commercial game. It runs offline in a desktop browser from static files, with keyboard and mouse, and all sound is synthesized in code.

## Run it

```sh
python3 -m http.server 8000
# then open http://localhost:8000/game/ in desktop Chrome
```

WASD / arrows drive and walk, E enter or steal a car, mouse to aim, click to shoot, SPACE handbrake, SHIFT sprint, P pause, M mute, C camera, F hail a taxi fare, H honk.
